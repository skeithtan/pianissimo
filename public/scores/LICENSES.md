# Bundled piano scores

Pianissimo can distribute these editions because their public-domain status or Creative
Commons license allows sharing. “Friendly Day” is publicly licensed under CC BY 4.0,
which allows sharing and adaptation with credit to Kevin MacLeod and a note about any changes.

Noctave published the editions below, apart from Pianissimo's transcription of Friendly Day.
Each entry records its source and license. The Noctave source bytes and embedded MusicXML
rights notices are unchanged.
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits copying,
modification, redistribution, and commercial use. The two scores under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) carry their own attribution
and share-alike terms. Scores under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
require attribution.

Pianissimo sets the library titles and composer names separately from the source metadata
and synthesizes playback from the notation.
